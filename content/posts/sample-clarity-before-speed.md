## Why clarity comes first

Teams rarely fail because they are slow. More often they move quickly in slightly different directions.

Before kicking off work, try saying three things out loud:

- **The outcome:** what "done" looks like.
- **The constraint:** what we will not trade away.
- **The owner:** who makes the final call.

## Try it this week

Pick one project and write those three lines at the top of the doc. Notice how many questions disappear.
