## Small promises, big signal

People watch how leaders handle the small commitments: the follow-up email, the five minutes on time, the "I'll check and get back to you."

When those land, the big promises are believed.

- Write down what you said you would do.
- Close the loop, even when the answer is "not yet."
- Say thank you when someone closes a loop for you.
