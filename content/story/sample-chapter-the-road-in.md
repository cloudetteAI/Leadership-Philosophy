## A chapter title goes here

Each chapter is a short, dated entry. Write it like you'd tell it at the dinner table: where you were, who was there, and what you took away from it.

## What I learned

- One lesson, in a sentence.
- Where it showed up later.

New chapters appear at the top automatically. Readers can also flip to **Read from the beginning** to follow the story in order.
